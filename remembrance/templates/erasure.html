WHAT ERASURE MEANS

What is removed, and what remains.

After review, the operator erases the life story, every stored photo, film and recording file, the timeline, tributes, candles, family access, reports, sharing grants, nominations and activity record. The memorial’s address and QR code stop working permanently.

A tamper-evident history remains: opaque account and record identifiers, times, fixed event codes and digests, but no names, stories or media. The operator also keeps a separate erasure record so that restoring an older backup cannot bring the memorial back.

Archives already downloaded, copies others saved, and content published elsewhere cannot be recalled. Server backups expire on the operator’s published retention schedule.
