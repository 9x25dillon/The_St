CLOSE AT HAND

A quiet place on your phone.

Remembrance works in your browser. You can also install it on your phone or computer, without an app store.

iPhone or iPad

Open this site in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen and Add.

Android

Open this site in Chrome. Use the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Desktop

In Chrome or Edge, look for the install icon in the address bar or the browser’s app menu.

Keep memories offline.

The installed app needs a connection to open memorials. Owners can download a complete ZIP archive from memorial care and open its index.html offline, including photos, audio, video, and stories.

Private profiles and media are never put in the app’s offline cache. Installation requires HTTPS, or localhost during development. Native iOS and Android store packages are not included in this release.
