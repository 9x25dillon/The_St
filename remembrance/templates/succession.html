WHEN CARE CHANGES HANDS

The memories stay together.

After an approved transfer, the new owner can care for and export the complete memorial, including private memories. Its address, content, family access and sharing permissions stay in place.

Your owner access ends when the transfer completes. Any family access recorded for your account is removed; the new owner can grant it again. You may still visit content open to the public.

This nomination records a preference and willingness. It does not verify estate authority, transfer ownership of uploaded works, or trigger automatically when a date of passing is entered.
